## 5. Why full-suite and focused results differ

The full-suite run reports 30/76 lines for `executor.c` (39.5%) and
48/134 lines for `pipeline.c` (35.8%). These values do not capture all
coverage generated by forked or executed child processes.

The focused F2/F3 remeasurement used GCC coverage-aware fork/exec hooks
and an exit wrapper to collect child-process coverage. It measured
73.7% for F2 and 64.9% for F3.

Therefore, the lower full-suite figures are a difference in coverage
collection scope, not evidence that the focused module coverage regressed.
The two measurement types are reported separately and are not directly
interchangeable.

F1's 79.7% is the combined `main.c` and `parser.c` full-suite result.
F4's 100% includes integration paths exercised by the complete suite.
